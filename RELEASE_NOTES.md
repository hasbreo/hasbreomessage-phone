# 1.0.0 · Your people, wherever you are

First Android release: full encrypted Hasbreo chat client with native sharing, camera capture, voice notes, Android notifications and replies, account-bound reply outbox, app lock, haptics, starred chats, persistent drafts, streaming downloads and explicit Wear OS companion pairing.

The same accounts, groups, history recovery and 25 GB file allowance are used across web and phone. Watch replies use stable transactions and keep waiting replies until acknowledged.

First-release preview pending physical-device testing. Background delivery uses optional Connected mode, with an ongoing notification and battery cost; this is not FCM push. Force-stop, network loss and Android restrictions can delay delivery. Calls and standalone watch networking are not included. Full-size 25 GB transfers have not been tested.
